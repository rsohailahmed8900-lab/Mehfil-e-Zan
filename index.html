Our Story

A Sanctuary Created by Women, for Women

In the heart of bustling Karachi, Mehfil-e-Zan ("A Gathering of Women") was born out of a simple need: a safe, elegant, and peaceful space where women can sit comfortably, share chai, work without intrusion, and foster meaningful sisterhood.

We are more than just a cafe. We are a sanctuary for students studying for exams, freelancers building careers, mothers looking for a brief respite, and creative souls eager to learn new skills.

Our Vision

To be Karachi’s most trusted and beloved sanctuary where women gather, grow, and glow together.

Our Mission

To serve comforting food and offer safe, accessible spaces and programs that empower women to learn, earn, and connect.

The Mehfil Difference

A unique blend of artisanal tea lounge, quiet work library, skill-building workshop studio, and women's membership network under one roof.
